DRIVER SAFETY & VERIFICATION

Why We Verify Every Driver

At Ahmedabad Airport Cab, your safety and comfort come first. Before a driver is allowed to accept a single booking, they go through a multi-step verification process covering identity, driving history, vehicle condition and on-road conduct. It isn't a one-time formality — drivers are re-checked periodically and monitored on an ongoing basis, so the standard doesn't slip over time.

6-Step Verification Ongoing Monitoring Rider-Rated Drivers

Our Verification Process

Every driver on our platform passes through these six checks before — and after — they start taking bookings.

1

Identity & Document Check

Aadhaar/PAN, address proof and a valid driving licence are verified before onboarding — no driver goes live without complete paperwork.

2

Background & Police Verification

Every driver goes through a background and police verification check to help keep passengers safe on every trip.

3

Vehicle Inspection

Registration (RC), insurance, PUC and the overall condition of the cab are inspected to confirm it's safe and roadworthy.

4

Training & Orientation

Drivers are briefed on safe driving, customer courtesy and route familiarity across Ahmedabad and Gujarat before their first ride.

5

Trial Rides & Rating

New drivers start with closely tracked trial trips, and every ride afterwards can be rated by the passenger.

6

Ongoing Monitoring

Documents are reviewed periodically and driver conduct is tracked on an ongoing basis, with follow-up on rider feedback.
